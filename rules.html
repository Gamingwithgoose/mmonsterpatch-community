Community Rules

Last updated: August 2026

Be respectful

No harassment, hate speech, threats, targeted abuse, or unwanted sexual content.

Keep the community safe

No scams, malware, credential theft, doxxing, ban evasion, exploit instructions, or attempts to disrupt the website or MMOnsterpatch server.

Post responsibly

Use accurate titles and context, avoid spam, respect intellectual property, and mark spoilers or sensitive content appropriately.

Protect privacy

Do not publish another person's private information or private messages without permission.

Use reports appropriately

Report content in good faith. False or retaliatory reports may result in moderation action.

Enforcement

Moderation can include warnings, content removal, feature restrictions, temporary suspension, or permanent bans from the community and connected Online Play services.
