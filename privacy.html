Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What this site stores

MMOnsterpatch Community stores the account information you provide, login sessions, profile preferences, linked MMOnsterpatch character information, posts, comments, reactions, friend connections, saved items, reports, and private messages.

Game account connection

Your community account is also your MMOnsterpatch Online Play account. The site reads approved character details from the MMOnsterpatch server, including save slot, character name, appearance settings, and competitive rank.

Private messages

Messages are visible to conversation participants. Server administrators may access stored data when required for abuse investigations, security, recovery, or legal compliance. Do not use the service for highly sensitive information.

Security and logs

The service may retain IP addresses, login timestamps, operational logs, and moderation records to protect accounts and operate the server.

Deletion and questions

Account deletion, export, retention periods, and a contact email must be finalized before launch. Add the correct contact address here.

Independent project

MMOnsterpatch Community is an independently operated fan community and is not affiliated with unrelated projects or rights holders.
